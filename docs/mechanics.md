This document serves as a foundation for core game mechanics and describing the environment the player finds himself inside the Underworld.

Note: athough described in the document, prayers cannot be utilized in the Odysseus challenge, as per rules of the challenge.



Odysseus challenge rules:

* No prayers available, shrines cannot be interacted with
* Shrines can be used to protect the player from archers' and wizards' line of fire
* All enemies present on screen must be eliminated
* Although 3 HP are available from the beginning, player cannot take a single point of DMG



###### MOBS:



Player:

* Health: 3 HP
* Stamina: 100%
* Movement: 1 hex
* Attacks:

  * Stab: 1 adjacent hex
  * Lunge: 1 hex, 2 hexes away in a straight path from player
  * Spear throw: 1-2 hexes away in any direction from player



* Abilities:

  * Jump: jumps to any hex 2 hexes away in any direction from player, drains 50% of stamina
  * Bash: pushes any enemy or bomb directly adjacent to the player by 1 hex in any direction, recharges after 3 turns
  * Pray: interacting with an altar of Apollo allows for upgrading player's stats and abilities



Demon footman:

* Health: 1 HP
* Movement: 1 hex
* Attack: melee attack, attacks the player only when directly adjacent to itself
* Rate of attack: 1 per turn
* Range of attack: 1 hex
* Notes: x



Demon archer:

* Health: 1 HP
* Movement: 1 hex
* Attack: ranged attack, shoots arrows in 6 directions in a straight line
* Rate of attack: 1 per turn
* Range of attack: 4 - 6 hexes depending on depth
* Notes: cannot attack the player when directly adjacent to them, will move out of the way to get the player in range



Demon demolitionist:

* Health: 1 HP
* Movement: 1 hex
* Attack: ranged attack, throws bombs which deal 1 DMG to any mob, player or enemy, directly adjacent to it's hex
* Rate of attack: 1 per 3 turns
* Range of attack: 3 hexes in any direction
* Notes: demolitionist will never throw a bomb on a hex directly adjacent to itself and will retreat to safety if it cannot throw a bomb at the player. If no safe space is available, demolitionist will freeze until able to throw a bomb or retreat



Demon wizard:

* Health: 1 HP
* Movement: 1 hex
* Attack: ranged attack, shoots beams of fire in 6 directions in a straight line
* Rate of attack: 1 per 2 turns
* Range of attack: 5 hexes
* Notes: unlike archers, wizards can damage the player directly adjacent to their hex, although sometimes when leaping to a hex adjacent to them, they sometimes do not fire and run for safety. After attacking, a wizard cannot attack on the next turn and needs to recharge his staff with fire.



###### TERRAIN:



Ground:

  * Plain, walkable hex



Magma:

  * Unwalkable hex, can sometimes form islands by encircling ground hexes



Altar of Apollo:

  * A shrine which allow the player to receive one out of 4 or 5 available upgrades



Stairs:

  * Hex which upon landing on takes you to the next depth



Golden Fleece

  * Hex containing the Golden Fleece needed to complete the game. The player cannot proceed further down the Underworld or complete the game without acquiring it, appears on depth 16



Portal:

  * Hex which the player needs to land on after acquiring the Golden Fleece to win the game, appears on depth 16
